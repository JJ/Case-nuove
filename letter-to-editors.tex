Dear Editors:

Please find attached our revised manuscript entitled "Between Cohesion and Division: The Early Modern Venetian Patriciate".

In this paper, we apply social network analysis to a multidisciplinary topic,
namely, the history of the Republic of Venice, analysing a dataset of patrician
marriages in order to understand the historical dynamics of the elections of
doge in Early Modern Venice, up to the 17th century, when a relatively small
group of families monopolized the highest job in the Republic.

Since the main point of the paper is reached through social network analysis,
i. e., using node, community, and network level measures to understand the
evolution of the network and its historical outcomes, we think that your journal
is an excellent venue for this research.

Yours sincerely,

Dr.essa M. Cristina Molinari, Dr. JJ Merelo
